ALL I WANT FOR CHRISTMAS IS A HOME OF MY OWN...." 🐾🎄

Hi, everyone my name is Thunderpaws! I'm a handsome, 7-year-old former street cat who has been waiting patiently for my big break. Despite a few appeals, I haven't had any interest yet - and to be honest, I'm not sure why!


I’ve come such a long way since my street days. Here’s a little bit about me:


🍲 Food Enthusiast: I love my meals!


💖 Affectionate Companion: I adore gentle strokes and will happily purr away to let you know how happy I am.


🎾 Playful Spirit: I’m just starting to rediscover the joy of playtime again!


As you can see, I am a very handsome boy with so much love to give. I’m looking to be an indoor/house cat where I can feel safe, cozy, and cherished. My ultimate Christmas wish is to have my own home, my own presents under the tree, and a family to call my own.


Could you be the one to give me the loving home I deserve? Please share my story to help me find my forever family! 🏠✨


Love and purrs in the hope that my Christmas wish comes true.

CHARLIE

Charlie, who is a lovely natured boy, is really upset as no-one has come to visit him yet. 😿


He has come on in leaps and bounds whilst living with his fosterer. He sits and chats to her and will tell her when his food is due, also when it's time to go to bed.


This boy has lived on the streets for years, has been abused by teenagers but has also seen kindness from people who have fed him. He will always give a head butt to say thank you for his meal.


He is perfectly healthy, had dental treatment, neutered, fully vaccinated  micrichipped and received flea and worm treatment.

Charlie would be best suited to live as an indoor cat or live in a home with a catio or cat proofed garden. He would make a perfect companion cat or could live as a family cat.
